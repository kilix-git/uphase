# 2026-11-15 - PZEM proof of concept complete, repo public

## Done

- **`v0.1-pzem-poc` is complete.** The PZEM-004T v3 + CP2102 proof of concept
  is built, tested and working on the uConsole. The parts were tested from
  2026-11-02 onwards, so the 2026-11-06 entry ("on order") was already out of
  date when it was written.
- **pzemlyzer is complete:** a menu-driven terminal power analyzer for the
  PZEM-004T, in [host/pzemlyzer-poc/](../../host/pzemlyzer-poc/). It grew out
  of the plain `pzem.py` reader. Main features:
  - live U, I, P, PF, f and energy
  - zoomable trend graphs (Braille), with the zoom shared across all values
  - energy bar chart per time interval
  - long runs stay small: full resolution for the last 24h, 1min min/avg/max
    after that
  - autonomous CSV logger with interval, start and end time, and min/avg/max
    per interval
  - software calibration (±20% max) and CT pass count
- Repository published at <https://github.com/kilix-git/uphase> (the project
  was renamed from "analyzer" to "uphase"). Tag `v0.1-pzem-poc` set and the
  milestone closed.
- Issues #1-#6 and the project board set up for the open questions.

## Measurements

Reference: Metrel 3309 (certified and calibrated). Loads: kettle (resistive,
~2.3kW) and a 230V/20W halogen lamp.

- **Straight single conductor, kettle:** PZEM 9.64-9.76A vs Metrel
  9.74-9.80A, within about 1%. P 2297W, PF 1.00. Reads exactly 0.000A with no
  load.
- **Linearity sweep, 1-10 wraps** (up to ~96A through the CT): the reading is
  (wraps + 1) × 9.62-9.64A for every run, about 1.2-2% low and linear. At
  105A, above the 100A CT rating, it drops another ~1%.
- No calibration was needed for normal use.
- Raw data was not saved in `measurements/raw/` for these runs.

## Problems

- **Counting turns:** wrapping the lead around the CT gives wraps + 1 passes
  through the core, because the straight pass counts too. The CT setting in
  pzemlyzer must be the number of passes.
- **Corrupt frames:** one frame read U=2786V even though its CRC passed.
  Garbage also appears at PZEM power-up and switch-off. `check_plausible()`
  in `pzem.py` now rejects these.
- **Low-current error still open:** on 2026-11-02 the 20W halogen read about
  3.3× low. That test used many wraps and was done before the wraps + 1 rule
  was understood, and it has not been repeated with the Metrel in line.

- Front-end MCU is the **Pico 2 (RP2350)**, not bought yet; see

- Licence: Beerware for the whole repository.
- Box power: a third option, **battery in the box**, was added to #1.
- **Standalone measurement** without the uConsole is deferred to #6. It will
  only be considered once the device works with the uConsole as its brain.
- Voltage transformers: five EN 61558-2 candidates (4kV primary-secondary) on
  hand. The best one is chosen by frequency response, then 2 more are bought
  (#2).
