## Measurements

Reference: Metrel MI3309 (certified and calibrated). The load was a vacuum
cleaner with variable power: a phase-controlled motor, so the PF changes with
the power setting. The CT was left at 10 wraps = **11 passes** (it is not
practical to change without disassembly); pzemlyzer CT setting = 11.

PZEM values were logged once a second with timestamps. Ten power settings were
used. For each one, the Metrel I and S were read by hand once the reading had
settled, and matched to the PZEM readings from the ~5 s before. The Metrel
shows S in kVA (0.01 kVA steps) and I in A (0.01 A steps).

U was 242.0-243.4 V. Raw CT current was ~26-34 A.

- **Current:** on average the PZEM reads **+0.9%** above the Metrel (sd 0.5%,
  range -0.2 to +1.5%).
- **Apparent power:** the PZEM's U·I is +0.7% above the Metrel S on average.
  The Metrel's 0.01 kVA step is already 1.3-1.7% of these readings.
- **Error depends on PF:** at PF 0.95-0.99 the error is -0.2 to +0.5%; at
  PF 0.67-0.81 it is +0.8 to +1.5%. On this load a low PF most likely means a
  more chopped current waveform. So either meter could be handling distorted
  current slightly differently; this data can't tell which.
- **Compared with the kettle sweep (2026-11-15 entry):** the kettle read
  1.2-2% *low* at a similar raw CT current (~30 A). This vacuum load reads up
  to 1.5% *high*. Across both loads the PZEM is within about ±2% of the
  Metrel.

## Decisions

- **No calibration factor is added.** The remaining error changes sign with
  the load (resistive vs phase-controlled motor), so a single correction would
  improve one load and make the other worse.
