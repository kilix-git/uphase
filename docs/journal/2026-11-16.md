## Setup

- **Reference:** Metrel MI3309 BT (DeltaGT), in the power/functional test
  mode. The load is plugged into its test socket. It shows S in kVA
  (0.01 kVA steps) and I in A (0.01 A steps). It does not show P or PF.
- **pzemlyzer:** the CT was left at 10 wraps = **11 passes** (it is not
  practical to change without disassembly); CT setting = 11. Raw CT current
  is therefore 11 × the load current.
- PZEM values were logged once a second with timestamps. Each Metrel value
  was read by hand from the display once it had settled, and matched to the
  PZEM readings from the few seconds before.
- Ambient temperature: 22 °C. Mains 241.4-244.4 V.

## Reference accuracy

This sets how much the comparison can tell us.

- **Calibration certificate** (Metrel, dated 2024-07-09; the scan is kept
  out of the repo). Relevant points:

  | Function | Applied | Reading | Limits |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Power / functional test | 0 kVA | 0.00 kVA | 0.00-0.03 kVA |
  | Power / functional test | 0.46 kVA | 0.46 kVA | 0.41-0.51 kVA |
  | Voltage | 230 V | 230 V | 223-237 V |
  | Clamp current | 90 mA | 90.0 mA | 85.0-95.0 mA |
  | Clamp current | 900 mA | 922 mA (+2.4%) | 850-950 mA |
  | Clamp current | 9 A | 9.23 A (+2.6%) | 8.50-9.50 A |

  The **test-socket load current** (the I used here) is not on the
  certificate as a separate item. Only the kVA reading is checked, at one
  point, with ±0.05 kVA (±11%) limits.
- **Manual:** current is true RMS, ±5% + 5 digits. The mA-resolution ranges
  in that table match the clamp input. The test-socket reading has 0.01 A
  steps. At ~3 A that is ±0.20 A (±6.7%), and at 0.27 A it is ±0.06 A
  (±24%).
- **Own consumption:** with only the pzemlyzer box plugged in, the Metrel
  shows 0.01 kVA, 0.03 A. The PZEM's own supply does not pass through its
  CT (the PZEM reads 0.000 A), so the Metrel sees ~0.03 A that the PZEM does
  not. It is mostly reactive and distorted, so it adds to the load as a
  vector, not arithmetically. It is negligible at 3 A and PF 1, but it
  matters for the small loads.

**Conclusion on the reference:** the Metrel can only confirm the PZEM to a
few percent at ~3 A, and much less at low current. The differences below are
**differences between the meters**, not PZEM errors.

### Results

- **The PZEM is within the Metrel's stated accuracy at every point**: vacuum,
  toaster, LED, charger, and the kettle sweep from 2026-11-15.
- **The low-current question from 2026-11-02 is answered:** at 0.062 A
  (0.68 A raw through the CT), the PZEM reads 7.8 W for an 8 W lamp. There is
  no 3.3× error. The old halogen result was most likely a setup problem from
  before the wraps + 1 rule was understood. The halogen itself is no longer
  available.
- **The difference grows with distortion:** toaster (sine) -0.2%, vacuum at
  high PF ~0%, vacuum at low PF +1.5%, charger +6-7%. This is suggestive, but
  it is inside the Metrel's tolerance, so it is not evidence of a PZEM error.
  Settling it would need a better reference, or a known distorted test
  current.

## Decisions

- **No calibration factor is added.** The reference is not good enough to
  justify one, and the remaining differences change sign with the load.
- The Metrel calibration certificate is kept out of the public repo; only
  the relevant numbers are copied here.
